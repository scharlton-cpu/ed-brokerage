Customs clearance

From arrival to release.

Clearance feels complicated because nobody explains it. We handle the paperwork, the duty math and the back and forth with customs. Here's what happens when you hand it to us, and who does what at each stage.

YouCustomerE&DYour brokerCustomsGrenada Customs
Start a clearance →
  1. You

    Send us your documents

    Snap a photo and WhatsApp it, or email it over. We'll tell you right away if anything is missing.

  2. E&D

    We classify and calculate

    Every item gets a tariff classification, and you get a duty and tax estimate before anything is filed.

  3. E&DCustoms

    We file and follow up

    We lodge your declaration and handle any questions or inspections from customs, with updates at every stage.

  4. CustomsYou

    Released and on its way

    Once duties are settled and customs issues the release order, you collect your goods, or ask us about delivery.

Estimates are based on the documents provided. Final duties and taxes are set by Grenada Customs, and processing times vary by shipment.

Car rental

Your island. Your ride.

Home for Carnival, here on business, or waiting on your own car to clear. Here's how you go from first message to open road.

  1. Pick your ride

    Compact for town, SUV for the hills, or a pickup for hauling. Tell us your dates.

  2. Book in one message

    WhatsApp us or use the form below. We'll confirm the car, the rate and the pickup spot.

  3. Show your license

    Bring a valid driver's license. Visiting? You'll need a local driving permit. Ask us how.

  4. Grab the keys

    We walk around the car with you, sign the agreement, and the keys are yours.

  5. Explore Grenada

    Grand Anse in the morning, Grand Etang by lunch, Grenville by sunset.

  6. Drop it back

    Return it on the agreed day and we check it in with you. Need more time? Message us.
